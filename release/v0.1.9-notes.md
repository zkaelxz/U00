# Baihe Studio v0.1.9

Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.1.9.exe` once on every PC, also on a copy that runs as the background service. Windows SmartScreen warns on first run because the installer is not code-signed. Later updates then install by themselves.

Back up your library before you update (Library > Library tools > Backup). The browser extension is updated to 1.2.19; Baihe keeps its folder (browser-extension in the data folder) current, so reload the extension once in chrome://extensions after the update.

## Watch
- Continue where you stopped, on any of your devices.
- Bookmarks per account, each with an optional note, and Undo after deleting one.
- A Next card offers the following episode, ready ahead of the end.
- Skip quiet jumps past an intro or a long silence.
- Each speaker's lines show in their own colour.
- Study shows a line's words with readings and meanings.
- Sleep timer, and swipe to seek on a phone.
- Watch together: phones on one account play a title in step.
- Cast to TV, and Save to phone, with the English-subtitled copy.
- Share one episode by a link that ends after 1, 3 or 7 days; End shared links stops it at once.
- Thumbs-down on a line, with optional better wording, teaches your style.

## Live
- A new watching view: stream chips, a lines box, a new-lines button and a Chat tab.
- Start card, viewing options and a TV mode with large text, full screen.
- Two streams at once, and Live starts ahead of waiting GPU jobs.
- A second person can join a stream that's already being captioned, including one started at the PC.
- Chinese starts on shorter chunks and shows its first subtitles sooner.
- Live-now cards and an auto-start switch for followed channels.
- Float the picture and captions in a small window when leaving the phone app; on mobile data the picture stays off until asked.

## Comics and Quick read
- Comic pages translate about twice as fast: three pages at once, with lettering done in the background.
- A hand fix of a box's English is remembered for the series and used on later pages.
- Translations no longer leave Japanese, Chinese or Korean words in the English.
- Quick read reads the right language, keeps names and pronouns per title, and letters every balloon in one face.
- Tap a bubble for its original text and a second translation; tap a page to flip between the redraw and the original art.
- Redo this page, zoom out, a brightness slider and warm night colours.
- Novel chapters in Quick read, saved to the phone for offline reading, and remembered so a re-read costs nothing.
- The next chapter starts translating once you're past the middle of this one.
- Comic reader: auto-scroll, low-data mode on mobile data, and sound-effect captions on by default.

## Grab and the browser extension
- Grab a whole series from its series page, with a time and cost warning first.
- Follow a series from Grab, bookmarks for sites and chapters, and pick up where you left off on any phone.
- Choose the size, outline and typeface of the English lettering.
- Grab blocks pop-ups, ad redirects and common ad networks.
- Pick pictures from the gallery, or send a video to Baihe, to translate.
- Long-strip comic readers now work in Grab and in the extension.
- The extension has a Quick translate switch (nothing saved), and its folder updates with the app.

## Find, Add and Library
- Find and Add are now one Find & Add page, with one-tap Add to Library and Comics, Novels and Videos filters.
- Library shelves: Ready, Needs you and Running.
- New sources: a Japanese novel site, a Korean webtoon site, and an unlisted novel site for pasted links.
- Pasted links to sites that need the extension say so clearly.
- Duplicate files are blocked, a duplicate titles finder is in Library tools, and an old source video cleaner frees space; restoring from Trash puts a video back on its title.
- Indonesian and Thai are new source languages, and Auto (any language) detects the language for you.
- Titles named from a link also get an English title.

## Jobs, people and settings
- The queue starts by itself; admins control it from a phone, and members can pause or cancel their own items.
- An activity log, a daily GPU chart, and a warning before hours-long transcriptions.
- People and access shows last seen, signed-in devices and a daily job limit per member; names can be edited.
- Members can add titles, bulk import and translate novels from a phone, with higher limits.
- Reading data, read marks and Quick video settings are kept per account.
- Settings marks cards a release added or moved.
- Benchmark Lab has the Quality tab, a one-tap blind comparison and score trends over releases.

## Phone app
- Phone alerts arrive while the app is closed, and a tap opens the exact page.
- A copied video or comic link is offered for subtitles or Quick read.
- A newer build is noticed while the app is open.

## Fixes
- The desktop shortcut no longer says another program has Baihe's port when Baihe is just busy.
- Long pages stop growing forever, with scroll boxes and Show more.
- Many small polish fixes: loading text, Retry and next-step buttons, and Undo in more places.
