# Baihe Studio v0.1.1 (DRAFT, not tagged)

The first release after the betas, and the first to ship from the stable line. Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.1.1.exe`, and check the download against its `.sha256`. The installer is not Authenticode code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns on first run. The `.sig` file is a release signature for the in-app update check only.

## What it does
- Transcribe (Whisper, with a hard-audio path for shouted or drunk speech), translate, review, export (SRT, VTT, ASS, LRC, burned-in video, EPUB) and manage a library of titles for Chinese, Japanese and Korean audio dramas, novels, comics and videos.
- Live captions, Scanlate for comic pages, novel and EPUB import, Discover and Sources, Jellyfin, a browser extension, and a job queue.
- Optional household access: Google sign-in, per-member rights, private-by-default sharing, an audit log, and a Caddy helper. Off by default; the app answers only on the PC itself until the owner turns it on.
- Three voice engines, used for novel reading only: OmniVoice, Qwen3-TTS and Chatterbox. Each installs from its own card in Diagnostics into a folder of its own.

## Known limits
- Browser-tier sources (Miaoqumh, GoDaManhua/Baozimh, Kuaikan, Zero-Sum Online, Bilibili Manga reader) are unverified on the owner's machine; Piaotian's Cloudflare challenge is stopped by design (use a saved page).
- Scanlate and novel import were tested on synthetic pages, not on a real comic or novel import through the app. Pages of a chapter that are linked by "next page" are followed (10 by default), also only on synthetic pages.
- The V100 needs the cu126 torch build; NVIDIA driver 528.33 or newer.
- The Listen API still defaults to OmniVoice when a request names no engine, and the model check before a narration always looks for the OmniVoice checkpoint, even when another engine is chosen.
- Not yet checked on a real run: the ML stack update (diarize, separate, SenseVoice, ML bubble detector) and a real Claude translation.

## Changed since v0.1.0
- Simple and Advanced views: a Simple / Advanced switch decides what the menu lists, audio, video and comic titles open on the Simple view, and Library, Translate, Review and the title page have simpler layouts (a paste-a-link start box, a checklist of steps, a riskiest-first view and a "next line to check" button in Review).
- Series: shared glossary groups that series inherit terms from, a cross-episode name-consistency report, series auto-run inside a spending ceiling, and a watch folder that turns dropped audio and video files into titles on the queue.
- Whisper and Qwen3 results are checked harder: lines are flagged where two engines disagree, the shakiest lines are reviewed first, credit-like lines are caught, and each line's end is extended to where detected speech stops.
- Live: captions held back by the picture delay, a GPU and Ollama sampler, whisper-ja for Japanese streams, and saving a session as SRT or text.
- Comics: a whole chapter is read once before its pages are translated, outlined lettering is erased using the art's own tone, and "Make test copy" gives an independent copy of a title for reruns. Speech balloons that were split into columns are joined and read in the right order, thought dots are no longer picked up as text, and text fits its balloon with fewer hyphenated words.
- Models: translategemma:12b and qwen3:8b are offered in the Ollama picker and local gemma4:31b is not, and Diagnostics lets you switch the Ollama default to another picker model (for example when Ollama cannot load Gemma 4); Breeze-ASR-25 is an opt-in Chinese Whisper model; Diagnostics > Models lists and frees cached GPU and OCR models.
- PC profiles: with a PIN set (Settings > PC profiles), the PC window opens on a "Who's using Baihe?" screen. Guest leaves out titles and series marked private, Owner needs the PIN, and Switch profile in the header goes back to Guest. It hides titles from view; it does not lock files.
- Backups: restoring a backup on Windows no longer fails because the app log was still open.
- Household access: admins can add a title or put one in a series from away, and members' paid spending is recorded and limited.
- Android app: full-screen video works in the WebView, Grab scrolls to load pages and follows next chapters, and Settings > App updates > Get the newest phone app works again (it was refused by GitHub with HTTP 415). The phone gets the new app after the server fetches it and the app accepts the update.
- Settings > App updates now offers stable releases without turning on pre-releases, and installs on a beta update to this release through the normal check.
