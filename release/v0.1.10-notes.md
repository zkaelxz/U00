# Baihe Studio v0.1.10

Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.1.10.exe` once on every PC, also on a copy that runs as the background service. Windows SmartScreen warns on first run because the installer is not code-signed. Later updates then install by themselves.

Back up your library before you update (Library > Library tools > Backup). The browser extension is updated to 1.2.20; Baihe keeps its folder current, so reload the extension once in chrome://extensions after the update.

## Live
- TV mode: the picture takes 70% beside the lines, with width presets, and text size buttons in the TV bar.
- Choose Fast or Balanced captions for each followed channel, and when a member starts Live.
- Give a followed channel your own name, and keep a word list for each one.
- Chinese uses the fast first chunk for saved settings and followed channels too.
- Check all channels sits at the top, and Stop sits under the player.
- On a phone, a drag on the lines scrolls the page.

## Comics, novels and Quick read
- Hold a translated comic page to see its original.
- A middle tap in the comic reader also hides the app header and the phone strip.
- A comic's Media tab shows its Pages; Scanlate buttons read Translate the rest and Start fresh.
- A built-in style note for historical Chinese novels.
- Quick read reads each picture once per run, and answers a page asked for again.

## Grab and the browser extension
- Grab has a Settings screen, clearer button names, and keeps your site sign-ins.
- Grab can keep going to the last chapter, and a shared link goes into its queue.
- Grab saves novel chapters from text pages; Cancel stops a novel Quick read.
- Grab and the extension pick up the chapter number, name and title details from the page.
- Bookmarks and recent pages screens, a sectioned More sheet, and history rows open their page.
- Long-strip comic sites open as their desktop page on the phone, with on-screen strips joined in the app.

## Phone app
- The bottom bar has a Live tab; Jobs moves into More.
- The top bar follows Baihe's theme.
- The PC fetches new phone builds itself, and the app has a Check for updates button.
- One-tap Report a problem from Grab, with a screenshot preview.
- The last crash shows on the next launch.

## Library and settings
- Admins see a GPU line next to the spent line.
- Report a problem includes more detail, with a Share button, and removes IP addresses.
- Titles that are only unsure no longer land in Needs you.

## Fixes
- The phone app no longer opens on a crash screen on some phones.
- Picture-in-picture no longer goes black or zoomed.
- Quick read lists no longer crash, and retry after a dropped connection.
- Live's player no longer opens Grab by mistake.
- Uploads are no longer added twice, and the title header no longer jumps.
- "Can't reach Baihe" no longer shows for checks that fail in the background.
- Many smaller Grab, comic and phone fixes.
