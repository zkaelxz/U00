# Baihe Studio v0.2.0

Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.2.0.exe` once on every PC, also on a copy that runs as the background service. Windows SmartScreen warns on first run because the installer is not code-signed. Later updates then install by themselves.

Back up your library before you update (Library > Library tools > Backup). The browser extension is updated to 1.2.23; Baihe keeps its folder current, so reload the extension once in chrome://extensions after the update.

## New look
- One shared look across the app: bolder side-menu rows, one thin line for admin warnings, and Settings, Library tools, Diagnostics, Benchmark Lab and People and access with their sections listed down the left.
- Settings sits last in the menu, below the admin pages. One You menu in the top bar holds view, theme, shortcuts, report and account.
- Library has a + Add button, four Continue tiles, one Filters menu, a Covers view and plain rows; the Ready and Needs you shelves are gone. One search per screen, also matching people, tags and series.
- A title's Add media, Redo transcription, Glossary and Search sit under a More menu. Small look fixes: Jobs' Activity log fold matches the Queue fold, "With originals" is a switch, warning bars are a soft yellow, the docked side menu has icons.
- Find & Add is one page with Add, Discover, Sources and Updates tabs; Text, Jobs, Live, Export and Transcribe got the new look, with the common options first.
- Title names use a cleaner font, and Traditional Chinese text uses Taiwan fonts.

## New
- Subtitles can show a pinyin or furigana reading line and karaoke highlighting, also on Watch and in the burned-in preview.
- Export a novel as a two-language EPUB, with optional pinyin or furigana. Export can add sound captions from the audio tags.
- Comic reader: read panel by panel, and read the English translation aloud in the Grab novel reader.
- Watch: lock-screen and headset controls, and a mark showing which ear a line comes from in stereo dramas.
- Library: a "Your month" recap of last month for each account.
- Queue: titles run side by side where it is safe, a Tonight item waits for the night hours, and a title's later steps wait behind its Tonight or paused step.
- Settings has a Send logs button that saves a scrubbed zip of logs, jobs and system info.
- Built-in VTuber name packs that Live uses by default, and Live can fix a name for the rest of the stream or speak the English aloud. Live says when the stream title looks like another language, and on an untitled stream short names are hinted only when said alone.
- Glossary terms match in either Chinese script.
- A Bell warning before a remote access key stops working.
- Admins can open private HTML pages from the app.
- Live: Add 60 min to a running session instead of restarting it.
- Listen: play the narration while the rest is still being made.
- The PC stays awake while jobs run.

## Phone app
- Every new build shows Update Available and waits for your tap; the quiet install that never finished is gone.
- A friendly Connecting screen with Retry, no black flash on first open, and every screen follows the app's look (light, dark, sepia, OLED).
- You can change the Baihe address in the app, and alerts keep running after a restart.
- Grab a novel's last chapter and the PC translates it, with progress shown; saving later chapters translates them too.
- Quick read: a "Reading N of M ready" bar, a Chapter ready notification, a steadier connection and a Copy button on the finished stats.
- Greyed-out buttons say why, and Grab explains an empty sites list and waits for slow pages to draw.
- The phone sign-in keeps its full length after the linking browser closes.
- Library's Continue button now sits small on the right of the tile.
- Quick read scrolls smoother on long chapters: the next page is decoded ahead.

## Comics and Quick read
- Quick read is faster, never drops or repeats a page, and reads tall strips in joined pieces so a bubble on a seam is read whole.
- Comic translation reads the whole page as a scene and rewords lines that don't flow. Pages are cut along their gutters for panel reading.
- The Korean text reader can run on the GPU with an optional install; more comic work moved to the GPU, and a GPU fault pauses it for 5 minutes, not until restart. Idle comic memory is freed.
- Cleaner balloons: no white notches, coloured dashes and glow lettering erased, white lettering in dark balloons taken whole, plain balloons painted to paper, even line lengths in lettered balloons.
- The comic page picture is sent as high-quality JPEG for Chinese and Korean pages, which is quicker; Japanese pages keep the sharper PNG.
- Jobs has a Quick read tab with Drop and Drop all.
- A comic run retries pages whose translation failed and fails if every page did; a retried chapter import adds only its missing pages.
- Screen readers hear a description of each comic page, and each new Live caption once.
- Dense comic pages keep their picture.

## Subtitles, translation and export
- Subtitle translation runs scenes side by side and cuts at long pauses. Lines a reply leaves out are asked again one at a time with their own neighbours, and a reply that shifted the numbering is refused.
- Reader: novel pages start at each chapter. Review's find and replace and search work on long novels.
- Novel chapters get their pronouns re-checked; divider lines become a scene break; more chapter heading styles are recognised on import; redo runs give each chosen line its own neighbours.
- Review stops flagging laughs and interjections as not real speech, scores each sentence from its own words, and offers the same English fix in the title's other lines.
- Transcription keeps its engine loaded between episodes of a batch, so later episodes start faster.
- Transcription is faster (the file is decoded once, Whisper loads while vocals are separated). Whisper also keeps speech the Silero v6.2 detector hears, and is primed with names on the first window only.
- Em dashes in English are replaced by a comma, colon or ellipsis, and kept where the source has one.
- ASS export colours speakers by character name. Export shows only the blocks that fit the title's media type.
- A slow or out-of-credit DeepSeek run moves to hosted Gemma at the next chapter, and when Gemma can't serve the error says DeepSeek is out of credit; a warning shows when its balance looks too low. Translate estimates price the Style and batch size the run uses.

## Library, titles and members
- Titles without a cover get one, at startup and on every Sources import; covers load as small cached thumbnails.
- Each member's tracked series stay private to them, capped at 100.
- Members can upload audio and video from their phone and download the extension, and see their own limits; controls the server would refuse are hidden or locked.
- Waiting jobs take turns between people, owner first; members' new jobs wait while the library drive is nearly full, and admins are not held.
- The owner is told when a member sends a problem report. Discord job cards name the device the job came from.
- The theme follows your account to any device.
- Backups warn once when the copies share the library's drive. The low-disk alert says how much a cleanup would free.
- Members no longer see the owner's backup warning.

## Changed
- Local Ollama models are removed: translation and Live use hosted engines only, and a saved local model name falls back to the hosted default. Hosted Gemma counts as a paid engine for limits and cost.
- Default Whisper silence length is 1000 ms with a 20 s speech cap. Many wording fixes across admin pages, Settings, Grab and the reader.

## Fixes
- The 8600 port keeps listening when a client drops mid-connection.
- Translation stops and says why when a retry hits a timeout or the daily limit; Grab queues stop when the monthly cap is spent.
- A long chapter that quotes an "empty page" phrase is no longer treated as empty.
- Faster storage scan, bulk delete and Options; fewer stale replies and errors on several screens.
- A vocal-separation choice made in the same save as a model change is kept.
- Live notices a dead stream link in about 15 seconds and has a scroll box and Hide controls for a stopped session's lines.
- The duplicate titles list no longer shows wrong "Same link" matches.
- Labels cut off in the Soft look now show in full.

## Extension
- A Video tab to subtitle the video you are watching or start Live on it; Baihe subtitles also show on the original video page.
- Clearer refusal reasons from the app.
- Scrolling carries on while a batch is translated, and late English is drawn on recycled pages.
