# Baihe Studio v0.1.2

Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.1.2.exe`, and check the download against its `.sha256`. The installer is not Authenticode code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns on first run. The `.sig` file is a release signature for the in-app update check only.

## Changed since v0.1.1
- Comics (Scanlate): the page picture is sent with the OCR text; the Ollama default is translategemma:27b (12b stays selectable); the text-only prompt asks for natural spoken English; art captions are on by default when available. Cleanup and typesetting: pale lettering and signs on art are erased without ghost text, a shaded sky is kept, a caption frame is kept, site stamps, site ads, page numbers and text that is already English are skipped, large regions are split when the lettering falls in separate blocks, words break at a syllable or their own hyphen, and balloons lettered alike share a size. Comic Neue is bundled as the default lettering face. Sound effects can optionally be glossed beside the art (off by default). A run can start on OpenAI (Luna) when its key works, and Diagnostics > Models has a Download button for the LaMa-manga cleanup model.
- Browser extension: translates with the page picture and the series cast notes and follows Scanlate's default engine; saved pages are cleaned and typeset (Typeset setting), and "Translate this page" draws the same clean redraw; capture continues to the next chapters for novels and comics, waits for slow lazy-loaded pages and says when the reader shows more pages than it found, shows elapsed time, and Cancel also stops the app's work on the batch in flight. A 403 on an image is retried with the page's origin as Referer. An optional screenshot fallback covers pages the browser will not let the script read. The title cleaner keeps the work's name when a chapter mark is in brackets and treats Korean and Japanese volume marks as chapter marks. An admin can add an extension device from the main PC, and the extension can be downloaded as a zip from Settings.
- Novels: prompts keep metric units, one form per name, gender from the source and the original prose's flow; the Instruction tab drafts a per-novel style instruction from a sample of the raw text; guardrails catch leaked CJK, changed numbers and carried-over headings; the post-check flags imperial units and drops invented speaker tags; existing translations are kept when a novel is split into lines again; a novel's title page shows per-chapter progress and a Read link, and tells which lines belong to which saved chapter.
- Glossary: terms are highlighted in the Reader (tap to edit, replace everywhere); word replacements apply to every translated line; finding terms drops terms not copied from the excerpt, needs evidence for gender, keeps ambiguity alternatives and accepts evidence quotes retyped with variant characters.
- Models: GPT-6 Luna, GPT-6.1 Terra and Sol are offered and the GPT-5 models are removed; Haiku 4.5 is removed from the Claude list; Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna have a "Less" reasoning choice; Benchmark Lab runs show a per-model summary table and earlier hand-run results; PaddleOCR is out of the Scanlate recommended install.
- Removed: Demucs vocal separation (Mel-Band RoFormer is the only engine).
- People and access: add a person from the main PC; a wrong PIN shows the server's sentence instead of a generic refusal.
- Smaller changes: the Simple steps column stays beside an open step on audio and video titles; a failed download says where to update yt-dlp; the Diagnostics model download no longer crashes on a disabled progress bar; the Android app has a launcher icon.

## Known limits
- Almost everything above was checked with mocked tests only (no real sites, keys or models). The Scanlate fixes #2335 to #2352 were also re-run on a handful of real comic pages on the V100 PC; the extension changes, the clean redraw, the screenshot fallback, next-chapter capture, glossary and novel changes were not run on real sites or real novels. The Android app and this installer build were not run on a device for this release.
- Site by site, the extension was tried by hand on Naver Webtoon, Kakao, Bomtoon, Bilibili Manga and Marumaru. Lezhin showed the copyright image on page 1, blacktoon failed with an image 403 (before the Referer retry), Ridibooks names the title wrongly and ComicWalker reads a blank page; none of these is fixed here.
- Narrow balloons can still break a long word with a hyphen (for example "insig-nificant"); the font is not shrunk to avoid it.
- The V100 PC (driver 536.25) cannot run local gemma4:31b; use translategemma:27b, Luna or Claude there.
- The limits listed for v0.1.1 that are not mentioned above still apply.
