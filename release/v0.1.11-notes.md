# Baihe Studio v0.1.11

Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.1.11.exe` once on every PC, also on a copy that runs as the background service. Windows SmartScreen warns on first run because the installer is not code-signed. Later updates then install by themselves.

Back up your library before you update (Library > Library tools > Backup). The browser extension is updated to 1.2.22; Baihe keeps its folder current, so reload the extension once in chrome://extensions after the update.

## Phone app
- Every new build now shows Update Available and waits for your tap; the quiet install that never finished is gone. The check also makes the PC look for a new build first.
- A friendly Connecting screen with Retry, no black flash on first open, and every screen and dialog follows the app's look (light, dark, sepia, OLED).
- Quick read: a "Reading N of M ready" bar that returns to the reader, a Chapter ready notification, a steadier connection while pages queue, and a Copy button on the finished stats.
- Greyed-out buttons say why, and "ask the owner" lines name the action.
- Grab says why its sites list is empty, waits for slow long-strip pages to draw, and its check boxes follow the theme.

## Comics and Quick read
- Quick read is faster on the PC and never drops or repeats a page; cover pages are shown as they are.
- Jobs has a Quick read tab with Drop and Drop all.
- Cleaner balloons: no white notches on outlines, coloured dashes and glow lettering are erased, and cut-short stamp lines are caught.
- A comic run retries pages whose translation failed and fails if every page did; a retried chapter import adds only its missing pages.
- Idle comic memory is freed.
- Comic translation reads the whole page as a scene and rewords lines that don't flow, for more natural English.

## Library, titles and members
- Titles without a cover get one: at startup for older titles with a video or comic pages, and on every Sources import.
- Each member's tracked series stay private to them, capped at 100; a tracked series keeps its new chapters and title link when it is tracked again.
- Members can upload audio and video from their phone, with a size limit, and can download the extension.
- Waiting jobs take turns between people, owner first, and members' new jobs wait while the library drive is nearly full.
- The owner is told when a member sends a problem report, and an admin sees the Library status line away from the PC.
- The theme follows your account to any device.
- The duplicate titles list no longer shows wrong "Same link" matches.

## Changed
- Local Ollama models are removed: translation and Live use hosted engines only, and a saved local model name is refused with a plain message.

## Fixes
- The 8600 port keeps listening when a client drops mid-connection.
- Translation stops and says why when a retry hits a timeout or the daily limit.
- A long chapter that quotes an "empty page" phrase is no longer treated as empty.
- Faster storage scan, bulk delete and Options; fewer stale replies and errors on several screens.
- Live: a scroll box and Hide controls for a stopped session's lines.
- The translation settings sweep also tries batch size 90.
