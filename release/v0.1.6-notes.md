# Baihe Studio v0.1.6

Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.1.6.exe`. Windows SmartScreen warns on first run because the installer is not code-signed. On 0.1.5 the update check is off by default, so press Settings > App updates > Check for updates to see 0.1.6.

Back up your library before you update: Library > Library tools > Backup > Back up now. The browser extension is now 1.2.11: unzip the new copy over its folder and press Reload in the browser's extensions page.

## New
- Novels translate up to 3 chapters at once on Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, a paid Gemini key or an Ollama hosted model, about twice as fast at about the same cost. Set it in Settings > Engines and keys > Translation style; 1 keeps the old one-after-another run, and a copy that already saved 1 keeps it.
- Translate a novel one chapter range at a time, with an optional single narrator.
- Review lists Chinese words that may be mis-heard, next to a spelling the title already trusts, with Listen, Use here, Use everywhere and Keep as heard. Transcribe shows how many it found.
- Review underlines the words Whisper was unsure of, on titles transcribed from now on.
- An optional second hearing listens again to doubtful lines and flags where the two engines differ.
- The Source-match check can show the lines it doubts to your AI translation engine, which confirms or clears each one, billed like the translation (a few cents per few hundred lines on Claude, less on DeepSeek or Gemma).
- Settings > Engines and keys > Translation style shows whether the bge-m3 model (used by the Source-match check) is installed on your Ollama server, with a Download button on the PC (about 1.2 GB). The download shows a progress bar (N of M MB) and a working Cancel; a cancelled download resumes next time.
- A missed-speech check runs by itself after every saved transcription.
- Doubtful lines can be redone with the stronger engine in one click, with the estimate shown first.
- Live reconnects by itself after a stream break, and carries on with the same captions.
- A Series folder list to browse the library by series.
- Title covers from a video frame, or from the stream site's own thumbnail for a link download.
- A link download can pick the video quality; it defaults to 1080p, with Best available and 4K still offered.
- Export can put speaker names in SRT, VTT and LRC subtitles.
- Optionally transcribe the next title on the queue while an online translation runs.
- A PC alerts card in Settings > Connections, and this PC's readings and alerts in Diagnostics.
- Library tools can typeset all comics again.
- Library tools > Voice bank: Add voice… keeps a voice from a 1 to 30 second clip on this PC, with no title needed.
- The voice bank has search, a language filter, Details to edit a voice's notes, transcript and language, and Replace clip at the PC.
- A series character remembers the bank voice given to it, so the same speaker in a later title gets that voice.
- Speaker sample clips are cut automatically after Detect speakers.
- A corrections report lists the source-text fixes made on a title.
- The Reader reloads the page as new English arrives during a translation, keeping your place.
- Narration clips are levelled to the same loudness, and a long book shows its progress clip by clip.
- Baihe Grab on Android has Quick read: the PC translates and typesets a page's images and the phone shows them one after another, with nothing saved in the library.
- The top of Settings and Diagnostics shows the version and its release date, and inside the phone app also "Phone app build N · built <date>".
- "Download the same video again (keeps the lines)" replaces only a title's audio and video; a file of clearly different length is refused.

## Changed
- Speaker detection uses Nemotron unless it can't run on the PC, and a new title starts with Detect speakers on.
- Notifications, the update check, resuming free-engine batches at start and vetted fast imports are on by default; paid batches still wait for you.
- Review fits the window on wide screens: the video sits beside the lines, the tools beside them from 1100 px, and only the lines scroll.
- Admins have full rights on titles and series at the PC, with an owner picker.
- Live translates a sentence split over several lines as one sentence, shows a sentence said twice in a row once, and warns before Start when the models won't fit the GPU.
- Translation memory learns only from lines marked Checked.
- Transcribe and translate from a title, a novel link or the Add box runs as a chain on the PC queue, so closing the page no longer stops it.
- Storage cleanup can remove separated vocals (Balanced and Minimal do), and they are removed when a title's audio changes.
- The Source-match check is now on by default (Settings > Engines and keys > Translation style). It needs the bge-m3 model on your Ollama server and does nothing without it; off-peak bulk runs are not checked. While it is on and bge-m3 is installed, a transcription waits for an online translation to finish, and the Jobs page says why.
- Claude translates 30 lines per request by default instead of 60, which lost fewer lines; you can set up to 90.
- DeepSeek and gemma4:31b-cloud now translate 90 lines per request by default (was 30), and the batch size limit is now 90 (was 60). Local gemma4:31b and Reflect stay at 30; Gemma stream titles still use batches of 10.
- Comic page pictures are now read by Gemma 4 31B on Ollama's servers (gemma4:31b-cloud) when the run uses a cloud engine (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek or an Ollama hosted model) and Ollama is running and signed in; otherwise as before (Claude reads it with Haiku 5.5). DeepSeek runs now get the picture too. A model on this PC never sends pages away.
- Separate vocals first is now on by default for titles that use Breeze-ASR-25 and off for every other model; it makes runs 1.5 to 5 times slower, and needs the GPU on and the separation pack installed. On the first start after the update, titles that showed Off are reset once to not chosen, so existing Breeze titles get it too; a title you switch Off afterwards stays Off.
- Working audio is stored as FLAC, about half the size; titles with WAV keep working.
- Made-up credit phrases are dropped from new transcriptions. A stock phrase like "thanks for watching" is kept when speech is heard within a second of it and dropped when it sits alone in silence.
- Header buttons are icons only, the text is a little bigger on wider screens, and phone buttons are smaller.
- The extension's long help text is behind (i) tooltips.
- A complete subtitle download by an admin marks the title Exported.
- The Shaky badge now reads "Unsure: N% sure".
- Discord notifications name the PC that sent them.
- Household members can play audio by default.
- Narration speaks two lines at once on OmniVoice and Qwen3-TTS (about a third faster), plays at 0.9 speed by default (Settings > System > Advanced > Narration pace) with the same gap between lines, reads a repeated chapter title once, and refuses a voice description OmniVoice can't use instead of going silent.
- When Nemotron detects speakers, the Expected, Min and Max speakers boxes are hidden, since Nemotron finds the number of speakers itself.
- Diagnostics shows the Benchmark Lab links only when Developer Mode is on.
- Settings > Sharing groups titles into Shared and Private, shows the Owner picker only once someone else has been added, and says items made at the PC belong to the PC owner.
- Live sessions on a paid engine with no monthly cap stop at a per-session spend limit ($2.00 by default, set on the Live screen).
- Live starts on TranslateGemma 12B on cards under 20 GB, and on 27B from 20 GB or when only 27B is installed.
- A voice OmniVoice picks for a speaker is saved on that character (and its series character), so new speakers in later chapters no longer change the voices of the others.
- Chunk and tag and Add chapters now wait for a translation or narration running on the title instead of stopping it.
- A pasted video link's "Make English subtitles" works on a fresh install, using a ready-made "Default" One-click translate profile.
- "Quick video translate" is now called "One-click translate" everywhere.
- The cost shown before Translate includes the Source-match check when it will run, as its own part of the price.
- Episode summaries default to Claude and the Ollama choice is gone (a saved Ollama choice becomes Claude); each line is sent with its speaker's name, and long episodes no longer come back empty.
- Live captions use 10-second chunks by default (was 20 s), so captions appear sooner. When the chosen model may not fit in graphics memory, Start asks "Start anyway?" once.
- One-click translate with a paid-engine profile says up front that a monthly spending cap is needed.
- A member's translation stops at their monthly limit for paid engines; a queued step on an Ollama cloud model counts as paid unless the person who added it saved their own Ollama key.
- Narration reuses each line's clip on the next run and removes clips no line uses any more. The first run after updating may re-voice more lines than changed, once.
- Messages that name a setting are links to it, in errors, job text, notices and the Queue.
- Settings: PC alerts are one row per rule and save as you change them; Settings shows how much of this month's spend came with imported titles (it still counts toward the cap); Advanced transcription shows only in Developer Mode.
- The title view switch is now called Guided / All tools, with a "‹ Title page" link back from a stage; the Sources page is titled Find; Library cards show covers.
- The Source-match check tells the engine the title's language, gives up after 5 seconds when the Ollama PC is off, and the finished translation says when the check couldn't run.
- Review: mis-heard word suggestions follow the title's script, the Unsure label says what is unsure, and there is a "Need a look" chip.
- Nine settings have new help text, including Episode summary engine and Default engine.

## Fixed
- Translation replies are checked more carefully before they are saved.
- The Source-match check now scores long lines (it used to skip the whole run without saying so) and flags English that fits a neighbouring line better.
- A local model no longer cuts a line off at a quote or blanks translations into Chinese, Japanese or Korean.
- One-line AI actions say why an engine call failed instead of showing a bare error.
- Listen says to install the pydub package in Diagnostics when it is missing, and a failed narration run shows its error instead of staying on "Starting...".
- Remember as a known character also gives the speaker the voice the series character remembers.
- Narrating a very long book (over about 24 hours of audio) now finishes the narration track instead of failing at the last step, without needing many gigabytes of memory or leaving large temporary files behind when it fails or is cancelled.
- An uploaded file with no audio in it is refused at upload.
- Speaker detection works on .m4a uploads and without a sign-in token.
- The Diagnostics "Speaker detection" pack installs what Nemotron needs. When neither Nemotron nor pyannote can run, the Detect speakers switch says what to install, and a transcription skips speaker detection with that reason instead of failing the step. Diagnostics flags a transformers version Nemotron can't use, with an Update button.
- On an NVIDIA PC without PyTorch, Diagnostics > Packages > Install for this task (and Install all) sets up GPU PyTorch first, then the task's packages, so the Speaker detection, Remove background music, Scanlate and Word timing packs finish in one press.
- Korean comics: the filter for site stamps no longer drops ordinary dialogue such as 토끼야!, 산토끼, 미리 보기 or 미리보기는.
- Chapters whose title ends in ?, ! or … are found, and a chapter title printed twice is saved once.
- A translate run with a spending cap stops before a batch that would go over it, and "Lines translated" is right after a retranslate.
- Off-peak scheduled translations stop before a batch that would go over the monthly cap.
- Importing titles from a backup brings each title's spend history, so the Library spend total includes it.
- A transcription can run beside an online translation when the Source-match check is on but its bge-m3 model isn't installed.
- The Source-match engine double-check now stays within the run's cost cap and the monthly cap (the finished job says when a cap made it skip the check) and works behind a fallback chain of Gemini, OpenAI or Ollama; cancelling a translation also stops the check.
- A job refused GPU memory as it starts now waits for the GPU instead of ending Failed.
- An outdated package can be updated at the next start, or now with Restart and install now (copies started with start.bat; a copy installed with Setup is closed and opened again, and a copy running as the background service says to restart the PC).
- A second Windows account can install and uninstall Baihe for itself with no administrator prompt, and the PC owner's background service is left alone.
- A slow but working connection can finish the installer download.
- A full disk while recording a finished update no longer rolls the update back.
- After an update restarts the app from a source copy, its restart log says whether the app stayed running.
- Uninstall removes all of Baihe's items in the Windows Temp folder.
- Manual backups are checked after writing, and an import compares every table it copies.
- Importing a backup skips a voice whose clip you already have, even under another name.
- Badges and switches stay readable in every theme.
- Many smaller screen, comic, novel import and Live fixes.
- Downloading a subtitle file in the original language only no longer marks the title Exported; only a file with the English (English or Both) does.
- "Use everywhere and remember this spelling" writes the spelling the suggestion showed, so a name's short form no longer turns into a doubled full name.
- The Video quality help no longer says subtitles come out the same at lower quality; keep 1080p or higher when subtitles are read off the picture.
- People and access no longer says to have someone sign in before they are added. It says to add them first, and the rights a new person gets include playing audio and video.
- Live warns before Start when the translation model (and Whisper, with Use GPU) may not fit in graphics memory, on default settings too.
- After a stream reconnects, Live's delayed player starts again, or the page switches back to the stream's own video.
- After "Replace clip" in the voice bank, the next narration speaks with the new clip instead of reusing the old voice.
- If more than half the lines of a narration fail, the previous track is kept and Listen says how many failed.
- A failed one-click run says what stopped in plain words, links to the job's details, and Continue picks up from the step that stopped.
- Step pages no longer show a second main button that only linked to the page you were on.
- While the Source-match check runs, the job says "Checking source match: N of M lines", and Cancel during it stops the job (the translation stays saved).
- Review explains Source-match flags in plain words, with the scores under Details.
- An app update that worked but couldn't record its version (full disk) now says so and still opens.
- The check after a manual backup shows progress and can be cancelled; a damaged backup says "The backup failed its check". The "newer library" error says to restore a backup made before you updated.
- Uninstall no longer deletes Temp work folders another running Baihe copy is using.
- A capped run started again after "Stopped at the spending cap" no longer sends one unpriced batch.
- A source line with a line break inside it is sent to the engine as one line; scheduled bulk and off-peak jobs read the novel file when they run instead of keeping a copy.
- A Breeze title no longer turns on Separate vocals first when the separation pack isn't installed, so a one-click run can't fail on it.
- A short network hiccup no longer ends a Live session; Stop while reconnecting ends it at once, and reconnects show as one note.
- The scheduled chapter check never uses the PC owner's signed-in browser for a series a member tracks; such series say they need the PC owner's browser.
- Comic read-ahead skips pages with nothing to translate; saving a chapter as CBZ refuses a chapter over 1 GB.
- A narrated novel's page turn in the Reader reads only that page, so long books turn pages quickly.
- Translate finds a novel's chapters from the novel text and refuses when a chapter heading was edited, instead of picking the wrong chapter.
- Chunk & tag speaker labels with "/" or ".." are cleaned, so tagged speakers can get a voice bank voice; a refused Listen start no longer changes the title's voice picks.
- Export never writes a raw SPEAKER_00 label, and in SRT a speaker's name counts toward the line width.
- Giving a title to someone is refused while it sits in another person's private series.
- The household sign-in message no longer mentions Google test users; turning adult sources on or off for two sites at once keeps both changes.
- Screen reader, contrast and phone layout fixes across Review, the Reader, Settings and the header.

## Security
- A guest profile no longer sees the PC owner's private titles.
- A site browser opens on the PC only for work started at the PC.
- Releases audit their dependencies before they are promoted.
- People who can't see a voice bank voice's source title no longer see its name or transcript.

## Known issues
- Benchmark Lab's batch-size sweep still tries only 15, 30 and 60 lines.
- A single title imported from an older backup keeps Separate vocals first Off.
- Korean comics: a short site-stamp fragment (4 syllables or fewer) alone on its own line is no longer filtered out.
