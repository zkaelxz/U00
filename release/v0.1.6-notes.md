# Baihe Studio v0.1.6

Install by running `BaiheStudio-Setup-0.1.6.exe`. Windows SmartScreen warns on first run because the installer is not code-signed. On 0.1.5 the update check is off, so press Settings > App updates > Check for updates.

Back up your library before you update (Library > Library tools > Backup). The browser extension is now 1.2.11: unzip the new copy over its folder and press Reload in the browser's extensions page.

## New
- Novels translate up to 3 chapters at once on online engines, about twice as fast, and can be translated one chapter range at a time.
- Review lists Chinese words that may be mis-heard and underlines words Whisper was unsure of.
- The Source-match check can ask your AI engine to confirm the lines it doubts, and Settings can download the bge-m3 model it needs.
- An optional second hearing re-listens to doubtful lines, and a missed-speech check runs after every transcription.
- Doubtful lines can be redone with a stronger engine in one click.
- Live reconnects by itself after a stream break.
- A Series folder list, title covers, and a video quality choice for link downloads (1080p by default).
- Export can put speaker names in SRT, VTT and LRC subtitles.
- Transcribe the next title while an online translation runs.
- PC alerts in Settings and Diagnostics.
- The voice bank can add a voice from a short clip, search, edit and replace clips, and series characters remember their voice.
- A corrections report lists the source-text fixes made on a title.
- Baihe Grab on Android has Quick read: translate a page's images without saving them.
- Settings and Diagnostics show the version and its release date.

## Changed
- Speaker detection uses Nemotron, and new titles detect speakers by default.
- Notifications, the update check and resuming free batches are on by default.
- Review fits wide screens, with the video beside the lines.
- Transcribe and translate run as one chain on the PC queue, so closing the page no longer stops it.
- The Source-match check is on by default; it needs the bge-m3 model on your Ollama server.
- Batch sizes: Claude 30 lines by default, DeepSeek and Gemma cloud 90; the limit is now 90.
- Comic pictures are read by Gemma 4 on Ollama's servers when the run uses a cloud engine.
- Separate vocals first is on by default for Breeze-ASR-25 titles.
- Working audio is stored as FLAC, about half the size.
- Made-up credit phrases are dropped from new transcriptions.
- Narration is about a third faster, plays at 0.9 speed by default, and keeps each speaker's voice across chapters.
- Live uses 10-second chunks, warns when models may not fit the GPU, and has a per-session spend limit on paid engines.
- Episode summaries use Claude.
- "Quick video translate" is now "One-click translate".
- Messages that name a setting link to it.
- Many screens have clearer wording and layout.

## Fixed
- Translation replies are checked more carefully, and local models no longer cut lines short.
- Spending caps stop a run before a batch would go over them, including the Source-match check.
- Very long narrations finish without running out of memory.
- Speaker detection works on .m4a files, and a missing package is named instead of failing the step.
- Diagnostics installs GPU PyTorch first, so package packs finish in one press.
- Updates, installs, uninstall and backups are more reliable.
- Novel chapters are found more reliably and never mixed up.
- Export never writes a raw SPEAKER_00 label.
- Many smaller screen, comic, novel, Live, phone and accessibility fixes.

## Security
- A guest profile no longer sees the PC owner's private titles.
- A site browser opens on the PC only for work started at the PC.
- Releases audit their dependencies before they are promoted.
- People who can't see a voice's source title no longer see its name or transcript.

## Known issues
- A single title imported from an older backup keeps Separate vocals first Off.
- Korean comics: a short site-stamp fragment alone on its own line is not filtered out.
